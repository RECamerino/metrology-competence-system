# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), which is incorporated here by reference. A verbatim copy is distributed at `docs/legal/contributor-covenant-2.1.md`.

In short: participate in a way that makes other people want to keep participating. Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments, and discriminatory conduct are not tolerated, in any project space, in any medium.

## What this means here specifically

Metrology is a field where being wrong in public is part of the job — uncertainty budgets get torn apart, methods get challenged, and that is how the work gets better. Rigorous technical criticism is welcome and expected.

The line is between the work and the person:

- **Fine:** "This budget omits the correlation between the two thermometer readings, so the combined uncertainty is understated. See JCGM 100 §5.2."
- **Not fine:** "Anyone who ships a budget like this shouldn't be doing metrology."

Two situations deserve particular care in this project:

**Reviewers and candidates.** A reviewer holds real power over someone's credential, and often over a newcomer with no professional network. Review firmly, review honestly, and review kindly. Dismissiveness toward someone earlier in their career is a conduct issue, not a style preference — and it directly undermines the project's purpose of lowering the barrier to entry.

**Credential holders.** Do not use someone's credential profile — what they hold, what they lack, how they earned it — to belittle them. Gaps are the normal state. The corpus is explicitly designed to exceed any individual.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the stewards named in `docs/stewards.md`. Reports are handled confidentially. If a report concerns a steward, send it to any other steward, who will handle it without that person's involvement.

Retaliation against someone who reports in good faith is itself a serious violation.

## Enforcement

Stewards will investigate and respond. Consequences scale with severity and pattern: a private word, a public correction, temporary suspension from project spaces, removal of maintainer or reviewer standing, or permanent exclusion.

Loss of reviewer standing on conduct grounds is recorded in the reviewer program (`docs/06-reviewer-program.md`) but does **not** revoke competency credentials the person earned. Competence and conduct are separate findings, and conflating them would corrupt the credential.

## Scope

Applies to all project spaces — repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, Commons instances operated by the project, and any space where someone is representing the project.
