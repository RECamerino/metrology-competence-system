# CM-17 — Teaching, Training and Knowledge Transfer

[← All domains](README.md)

> Developing other people's competence. Load-bearing for this project: attaining Level 5 in any element requires demonstrated mentoring of others at Level 3 or above, on the view that you have not mastered something until you can bring someone else to competence in it. Also the domain that addresses the field's succession problem. Boundary: assessing competence formally is CM-11; communication craft is CM-15.
