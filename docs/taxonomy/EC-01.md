# EC-01 — Electrical Test and Measurement Instrument Calibration

[← All domains](README.md)

> Calibrating the instruments that measure electrical quantities, organised by the instrument that arrives on the bench rather than by the quantity it measures. Oscilloscopes, multimeters, sources, generators and analysers each present a whole job: several parameters, several standards, and failure modes that belong to the instrument rather than to any one quantity. Boundary: the physics and the measurement science are DP-08, DP-09 and DP-14; calibration methodology is CM-06; this pack is the work.
