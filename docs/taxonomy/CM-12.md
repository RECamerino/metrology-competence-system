# CM-12 — Laboratory Operations, Environment and Facilities

[← All domains](README.md)

> The physical and operational conditions that make good measurement possible. Frequently treated as facilities management and therefore delegated away from metrology, which is how laboratories end up with capability limited by their own building. Boundary: the accreditation clause requirements are CM-10; safety is CM-22.
