# DP-17 — Biological, Clinical and Medical Metrology

[← All domains](README.md)

> Measurement on living systems and on samples taken from them, where the measurand may be biologically variable, the reference material may not be commutable, and the consequence of error is clinical. Includes biological quantities whose "unit" is a conventional international unit rather than an SI unit — a genuine metrological anomaly worth understanding. SCOPE NOTE: several professional disciplines in one pack, not a syllabus; no individual is expected to hold all of it, and role profiles select from it rather than assume it entire.
