# CM-22 — Human Factors, Safety and Risk

[← All domains](README.md)

> The person in the measurement loop, and the hazards of the work itself. Human factors are included because operator effects are a genuine uncertainty source that budgets routinely under-represent, and because a metrologist who is injured or fatigued measures badly. Boundary: measurement decision risk is CM-08; management-system risk is CM-10.
