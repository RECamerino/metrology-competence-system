# DP-15 — Ionizing Radiation, Dosimetry and Nuclear Instrumentation

[← All domains](README.md)

> Measurement of ionizing radiation and the dose it delivers. Unusual in that the measurand of greatest interest — absorbed dose in tissue — is often not directly measurable, so the discipline is built on carefully justified chains of conversion. Counting statistics also give it a distinctive uncertainty treatment. Boundary: radiation safety practice is CM-22-A04.
