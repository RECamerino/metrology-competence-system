# CM-08 — Measurement Decision Risk, Guardbanding and Conformity Assessment

[← All domains](README.md)

## CM-08-A05 — Accuracy Ratios and Legacy Approaches

> Ratio rules remain contractually mandated in much defence and aerospace work. Practitioners must understand what they do and why the risk-based approach replaced them. Authoring constraint: Z540-1 and Z540.3 are both withdrawn as active standards yet still invoked by contract. Elements must state that status and keep three things separate — what the standard historically required, where it still binds contractually, and what ISO/IEC 17025 asks instead. Teaching a withdrawn requirement as a live one is the failure mode to avoid.
