## CM-07-A01 — MSA Fundamentals

> Authoring constraint for this whole domain: MSA vocabulary is NOT metrology vocabulary and must never be presented as though it were. Repeatability, reproducibility, bias, linearity, stability and resolution all carry meanings in AIAG, VDA, ISO and laboratory practice that do not coincide. Every element here states which framework it is speaking in — "in the AIAG sense" — and where that framework diverges from VIM and GUM usage, the element says so. An organization-agnostic BOK that quietly adopts one sector's dialect as the truth has failed at its own premise.

## CM-07-A07 — Measurement Assurance and Measurement Process Control

> Ongoing demonstration that a measurement system remains capable of producing trustworthy results between calibrations. Distinct from calibration, which is a point in time, and from MSA, which characterises a system once. Components of assurance appear across CM-06, CM-09, CM-10 and CM-12; this area is the discipline that composes them into a programme, and it is what an assessor is actually probing when they ask how you know your results are still good.
