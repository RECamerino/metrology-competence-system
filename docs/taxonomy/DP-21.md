# DP-21 — Geodetic and Gravitational Metrology

[← All domains](README.md)

> Measurement of position, height and the Earth's gravity field, treated as metrology rather than as surveying. In scope because optical clocks now resolve elevation change at the centimetre level, which makes the gravity potential a quantity a time and frequency laboratory measures — the boundary between geodesy and metrology has moved. Boundary: gravimetry serving mass and force realisation is DP-02-A06; GNSS timing is DP-14; large-volume dimensional work is DP-01-A05.

## DP-21-A05 — Relativistic Geodesy and Chronometric Levelling

> The reason this pack is in scope. A clock's rate depends on the gravity potential at its location, so comparing two optical clocks measures the potential difference between them — height determined by frequency rather than by levelling. Metrology and geodesy are becoming the same measurement here, and this area is deliberately weighted toward expert level because the field is still being built.
