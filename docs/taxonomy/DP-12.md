# DP-12 — Photometry, Radiometry and Colorimetry

[← All domains](README.md)

> Optical radiation measured physically (radiometry), as perceived by the human eye (photometry), and as colour. Unusual among disciplines in that one of its base units, the candela, is defined by reference to a human observer — which makes the distinction between physical and perceptual quantities a competence in its own right rather than a technicality.
