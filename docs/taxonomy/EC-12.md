# EC-12 — Medical and Biomedical Equipment Calibration

[← All domains](README.md)

> Calibrating and performance-verifying medical devices, where the measurand is a patient parameter, the analyzer simulates a physiology, and the acceptance criteria come from a clinical standard rather than a manufacturer's specification. Scope is the whole hospital inventory, not only what a laboratory receives on a bench — hence imaging systems, operating tables and in-situ safety testing. Boundaries: the measurement science is DP-17; calibrating the safety analyzer is EC-01, performing the test on a device is EC-12-A10.
