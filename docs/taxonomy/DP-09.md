# DP-09 — AC, Power, Energy and Impedance

[← All domains](README.md)

> Alternating quantities, electrical power and energy, and the impedance standards that underpin them. Distinguished from DC work by the fact that almost every quantity becomes frequency dependent and phase becomes a first-class measurand. Boundary: DC is DP-08; above roughly 1 GHz is DP-10; utility metering regulation is CM-19-A03.
