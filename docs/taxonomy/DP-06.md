# DP-06 — Humidity and Moisture

[← All domains](README.md)

> Water vapour in gases and water content in solids and liquids. A small discipline with an outsized error rate, because humidity quantities are interconvertible only through temperature and pressure, and practitioners routinely conflate them. Boundary: humidity as an influence quantity on other measurements is CM-12-A01.
