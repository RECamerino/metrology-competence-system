# CM-11 — Assessment, Auditing and Approved Signatory Competence

[← All domains](README.md)

> The competence to judge someone else's technical work — as an internal auditor, an accreditation assessor, or an approved signatory taking personal responsibility for a result. Distinct from doing the work well; many excellent metrologists assess badly. Directly served by the Assessor Competence Dossier feature. Boundary: what the requirements say is CM-10; this domain is the skill of evaluating conformance to them.
