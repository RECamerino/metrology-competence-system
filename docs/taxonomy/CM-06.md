## CM-06-A09 — Metrological Confirmation and Fitness for Intended Use

> Confirming that a specific item of equipment is fit for a specific measurement process, which is a different question from whether it was calibrated. A calibration reports what an instrument does; a confirmation decides whether that is good enough for the job it is about to do. The central concept of ISO 10012 and the step most often skipped, because a calibration certificate is mistaken for an answer to a question it never asked. Boundary: the management-system requirement is CM-10; the risk mathematics behind the decision is CM-08.
