# CM-19 — Ethics, Legal and Regulatory Metrology

[← All domains](README.md)

> Where measurement carries legal force, and where a metrologist's professional obligations may conflict with commercial pressure. Legal metrology (OIML and national frameworks) and professional ethics are combined here because both concern the enforceable consequences of a measurement result.
