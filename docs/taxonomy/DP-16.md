# DP-16 — Analytical Chemistry, Reference Materials and Gas Metrology

[← All domains](README.md)

> Chemical measurement, where the sample is usually destroyed, the measurand is defined by the method, and sampling error routinely exceeds analytical error by an order of magnitude. That inversion of priorities relative to physical metrology is the central thing a physically-trained metrologist must absorb. Boundary: spectroscopic instrumentation is DP-13; clinical measurement is DP-17.
