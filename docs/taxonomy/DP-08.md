# DP-08 — DC and Low Frequency Electrical Metrology

[← All domains](README.md)

> Voltage, current, resistance and charge at DC and low frequency, including the quantum standards that now anchor the electrical units. The discipline where the gap between what an instrument displays and what it measured is widest, because thermal EMFs, leakage and guarding operate below the level most practitioners think about. Boundary: AC power and impedance are DP-09; RF is DP-10.
