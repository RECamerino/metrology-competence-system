# CM-15 — Technical Communication and Documentation

[← All domains](README.md)

> Writing and speaking about measurement so that the right people act correctly on it. One of the most commonly missed competencies: a metrologist whose analysis is right and whose report is unreadable has not finished the job. Includes adapting technical content to an audience, which is a distinct and teachable skill. Boundary: visual presentation is CM-16; teaching is CM-17.
