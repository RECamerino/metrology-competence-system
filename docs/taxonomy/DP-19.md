## DP-19-A05 — Nondestructive Measurement and Inspection

> NDT treated as measurement science rather than as a set of trades. The distinctive competence is that the measurand is usually a defect that may not be there, so detection probability and sizing uncertainty matter more than instrument accuracy, and a negative result carries a claim that must be quantified. Individual physical techniques also appear in their own packs — ultrasonics in DP-04, eddy current in DP-08, radiography in DP-15, thermography in DP-05 — and this area is where they are treated as inspection methods.
