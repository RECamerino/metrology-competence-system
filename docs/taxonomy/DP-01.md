# DP-01 — Dimensional and Geometric Metrology

[← All domains](README.md)

> Length, form, orientation, location and surface texture. The largest discipline pack, reflecting both its breadth and the fact that geometric specification (ASME Y14.5 and ISO GPS) is a language in its own right that takes years to read fluently. Note the ASME and ISO treatments diverge in ways that cause real inspection disputes; elements must name those divergences rather than pick a side.
