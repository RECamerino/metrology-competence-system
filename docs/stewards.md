# Stewards

**No stewards are currently appointed, and there is no timetable for appointing them.**

That is a known and accepted state, not an oversight. Appointment needs people who do not yet exist in the project's orbit, and it is not something design work can unblock. What matters is that the consequence is stated rather than discovered.

[`GOVERNANCE.md`](../GOVERNANCE.md) reserves six classes of decision to stewards — the ID registry, the source licence register, post-freeze schema changes, the cryptographic suite, the trust registry, and the meaning of the proficiency levels. **Until this file names people, every one of those rules is unenforceable**, and changes in those areas are being made on one person's judgement without the review the governance document describes.

## The operating rule while this persists

**Design and authoring proceed. Issuance does not.**

| May proceed | Must wait |
|---|---|
| Schemas, validators, tooling | **Issuing any real credential to any person** |
| BOK articles, elements, items, modules | **Populating the founding cohort** (decision 43) |
| Changing steward-reserved semantics, recorded in the decision record | Admitting an issuer to the trust registry |

The distinction is that a design decision made today can be revised at no cost to anybody, whereas a credential issued today is a claim about a real person made under rules nobody ratified. Several steward-reserved semantics have already changed during design — decisions 43, 44, 46 and 48 — and each is recorded in [`00-context.md`](00-context.md) with its reasoning so that whoever eventually holds the role can accept, revise or reverse it with the argument in front of them rather than reconstructing it.

**Nothing in this system has issued a credential, so nothing has yet been decided that harms anybody.** That is what makes the delay tolerable, and it is also what ends the moment the first real signoff happens.

## What appointment requires

Stewards are named here, by consensus of existing maintainers, per `GOVERNANCE.md`. Before the first credential is issued the project needs, at minimum:

- enough stewards that no single person holds a veto or a rubber stamp;
- representation from more than one organization, for the same reason credentials require a cross-organizational signer at L5;
- declared conflicts of interest, since the field includes people who sell training, calibration services and software.

## Format

Each entry records the name, affiliation, declared interests, and the date of appointment. Affiliation and interests are recorded because a reader weighing a steward decision needs them, exactly as they need the reviewers on a BOK article.
