# Stewards

**No stewards are currently appointed.**

That is not a placeholder formality. [`GOVERNANCE.md`](../GOVERNANCE.md) reserves six classes of decision to stewards — the ID registry, the source licence register, post-freeze schema changes, the cryptographic suite, the trust registry, and the meaning of the proficiency levels. **Until this file names people, every one of those rules is unenforceable**, and any change in those areas is being made on one person's judgement without the review the governance document claims.

This matters most for the decisions that cannot be undone. A schema change made now, before stewards exist, will still be binding on every credential issued later.

## What appointment requires

Stewards are named here, by consensus of existing maintainers, per `GOVERNANCE.md`. Before the first credential is issued the project needs, at minimum:

- enough stewards that no single person holds a veto or a rubber stamp;
- representation from more than one organization, for the same reason credentials require a cross-organizational signer at L5;
- declared conflicts of interest, since the field includes people who sell training, calibration services and software.

## Format

Each entry records the name, affiliation, declared interests, and the date of appointment. Affiliation and interests are recorded because a reader weighing a steward decision needs them, exactly as they need the reviewers on a BOK article.
