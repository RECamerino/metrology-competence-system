# Rubric — ARC-0004, Construct an uncertainty budget from records

Hybrid. The combined value is auto-scored for internal consistency; everything
below is applied by a credentialed reviewer. Double-scored at L4 and L5.

| Component | Weight |
|---|---|
| The structural feature is handled, not merely present | 35% |
| Selection: what was included, and what was correctly left out | 25% |
| Judgements the records do not make are recorded with their basis | 25% |
| Combined value internally consistent with the declared model, units included | 15% |

## Score the model, not the number

There is no single expected answer. Two defensible models produce two different
combined uncertainties and **both are correct**; the numeric component checks
that the candidate's arithmetic matches *their own* declared model, not that it
matches the author's.

A reviewer who marks toward the budget they would have written has broken the
item. If you find yourself deducting for a defensible modelling choice you
disagree with, that is a note to the candidate, not a deduction.

## Inclusion is not the skill

The pack contains records that do not belong in the budget. **A response that
budgets everything supplied, correctly, scores poorly on the second
component** — usually around a third of the available credit for it. Deciding
what is irrelevant is most of the work, and a candidate who cannot leave
anything out has not made any decisions.

The mirror failure is worse and scores zero on that component: a contribution
that is genuinely significant at the drawn target, omitted with no comment.

## Credit for "this cannot be resolved"

Some draws set `resolvable: no`, and for those the honest answer is a reasoned
treatment with the residual assumption stated plainly, not a number.

**That answer scores full marks on the third component.** A candidate who
invents a plausible value to fill the gap has demonstrated the opposite of the
competence under test — mark it down even where the invented value is close,
and say why in the feedback.

## Level differentiation

Set by `inputs_supplied` and `resolvable` in the binding, not by this rubric.

- **L1** — values supplied, the structural feature pointed out. Credit is for
  applying it correctly, not for finding it.
- **L2** — values supplied, feature identified by the candidate.
- **L3** — records only. The candidate builds the model, selects, and records
  the basis. This is the level at which independent laboratory work is
  entrusted, and the standard should be applied accordingly.
- **L4** — the target makes an ordinarily-negligible contribution significant,
  or the feature interacts with another one. Expect the candidate to defend the
  treatment under questioning.
- **L5** — `resolvable: no`. No established approach settles it. A defensible
  treatment with the cost of the assumption stated scores full marks; so does a
  well-argued case that the target is unachievable with these records.

## Notes for reviewers

Where the candidate finds a real defect in the supplied records that the
generator did not intend, credit it and report the draw. A pack producing
unintended defects is a bug in the item, not a bonus for the candidate.
