summary: >-
  Selecting a distribution where the source statement genuinely admits more
  than one reading, and holding the selection under challenge. The individual
  assignments each have their own element and their own divisor; this is the
  competence that sits above them, and it is exercised whenever a datasheet
  states a limit and describes typical performance on the same page. The
  profession has not settled the manufacturer-specification case, which is why
  the element reaches 5: what is assessed at the top is not the right answer
  but a defensible position and an honest account of what would overturn it.
levelCeiling: 5
anchors:
  1: >-
    Given two candidate shapes for one source statement and told what
    distinguishes them, decides which the statement supports, and says what the
    other would have implied about where the quantity lies within the interval.
  2: >-
    For a supplied source statement, selects between a rectangular assignment
    and a normal one recovered from a coverage claim, and states the effect of
    the selection on the contribution and in which direction it moves the
    reported uncertainty.
  3: >-
    For a source they have not met before, selects the shape, produces the
    line, and records the reading of the source wording the selection rests on
    — so that a reviewer can disagree with the reading rather than only with
    the number, which is what makes the selection reviewable at all.
  4: >-
    Holds a position on a source statement that genuinely admits two readings,
    defends it against a reviewer arguing the other, concedes the points in the
    counter-argument that are sound rather than defending the whole of an
    initial position, and states what in the source would change the selection.
  5: >-
    Resolves a selection where practice is divided and the literature does not
    settle it — a cyclic influence where arcsine and rectangular are both
    defended in the field — sustains the position under informed challenge,
    names the evidence that would decide it rather than asserting the field is
    agreed, and brings another practitioner to the point of making and
    defending the same call unaided.

Notes for assessors and item authors. The subject is explained in `BOK-0002`,
principally §s05 — do not restate it here.

**This is a `judgment` element and the anchors describe decisions defended, not
divisors applied.** Applying a divisor once the shape is settled belongs to
`CM-03-036`, `CM-03-037`, `CM-03-038` and `CM-03-039`. What is assessed here is
the selection between them where the source genuinely supports more than one,
and an item whose scenario has a clear-cut answer is testing one of those
elements instead.

**The bindings deliberately assign the candidate a side.** At L4 and L5 the
item shape puts them on a position they did not choose, which is the only way
to observe a defence rather than a preference. A candidate permitted to pick
their side will pick the one they find easier to argue, and the resulting
performance says nothing about whether they can hold the other.

**Two observable behaviours, and they are not the same.** Defending a selection
one is making, and recognising a wrong assignment already committed in someone
else's budget, are distinct — which is why this element carries a
defect-recognition binding at L4 alongside the position-and-defend ones.
Reading a budget and finding root two where root three belongs draws on
different work than choosing between them from a datasheet.

**Where candidates fail, in order of frequency.** Retreating to the divisor
when asked for the reading — restating what rectangular implies rather than why
this source supports it. Defending the whole of an initial position under
challenge, which the L4 anchor explicitly does not reward: conceding a sound
point is part of the observable behaviour, not a loss. Selecting the shape that
produces the wanted number and reasoning backwards. And asserting that the
field is agreed when it is not, which at L5 is the failure the anchor is
written to catch.

**The ceiling is 5, and the test in `docs/anchor-template.md` is satisfied.**
The arcsine-against-rectangular case for a cyclic influence is a selection a
competent practitioner can get wrong under informed pressure, and `ARC-0003`
binds an item to it. If the L5 draws ever settle into a case with a known
answer, the ceiling is wrong rather than the anchor loose.

**Boundary with `CM-03-035`.** Judging how far a manufacturer specification can
be trusted as a source at all is `CM-03-035`. This element takes the source as
given and decides what its wording supports. The two are adjacent and an item
can easily test both by accident, which is worth checking when a binding is
reviewed.
