summary: >-
  Turning a two-part accuracy specification into a permitted error at an
  operating point, and into the limits a reading may take. The arithmetic is
  one multiplication and one addition; the errors are all bookkeeping. The
  counts term is a different voltage on every range, the percentage is of the
  reading and not of full scale, and the figure produced is conditional on a
  temperature band, a warm-up and a calibration interval that are printed
  somewhere less prominent than the number itself.

  1: >-

    permitted error at that point and states it in the units of the
    measurement, carrying the units through both terms.
  2: >-
    For a supplied datasheet and a stated operating point, computes the
    permitted error from the specification, produces the upper and lower limits
    the reading may take, and records which of the specification's stated
    conditions the figure depends on — the temperature band, the warm-up
    period, and which calibration-interval column was read.

Notes for assessors and item authors. The subject is explained in `BOK-0005`
§s03 and §s04 — do not restate it here.

**A `skill` element at ceiling 2, which is an unusual shape and a deliberate
one.** `skill` means the evidence is a work product rather than an explanation;
it says nothing about the level the competence reaches. Here the work product
is a computed figure with its working, and there is genuinely no unaided-novel
case beyond L2 — every accuracy specification of this form yields to the same
two steps. The depth is elsewhere: choosing which specification applies is
`DP-08-081` and `CM-03-035`, and turning the figure into a budget contribution
is `CM-03-036`.

**The assessable artifact is the working, not the answer.** A single number
cannot be checked by anybody and an assistant produces it instantly. What
distinguishes a competent submission is that the two terms are computed
separately, the range is stated, and the units survive to the end.

**Where candidates fail, in order of frequency.** Taking the percentage of full
scale rather than of the reading — which gives the right answer at full scale
and is increasingly wrong below it, so it survives a spot check. Evaluating the
counts term with the resolution of the wrong range. Reading the 90-day column
for an instrument eleven months into its interval, which is the error most
likely to matter commercially. Dropping the temperature qualifier, so a figure
computed for 23 °C is applied on a bench at 30 °C. And producing a permitted
error without saying which range it belongs to, which leaves the number
undefined.

**Ceiling 2 and every role at 2**, per `DP-08-094`. This element also has no
prerequisites, for the same reason: it is entry to the discipline, and
requiring anything ahead of it would rebuild the barrier rule 13 removes.
